# Credits

## The engine

Tamanimals is built on the firmware engine of **TamaPoke**, an MIT-licensed open-source project. The
notices the MIT license requires are kept in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

- **Quique Tortosa** created it: [socquique/TamaPoke](https://github.com/socquique/TamaPoke) (firmware,
  UI, ES8311 audio bring-up, browser installer)
- **Dylan Dao**: [DylanPDao/TamaPoke](https://github.com/DylanPDao/TamaPoke)
- **Cheng Ren**: [crenberries428/TamaPoke](https://github.com/crenberries428/TamaPoke) (battle system, party
  and box, peer-to-peer link, save backup, synth)
- **Salchi**: minigames, shop, coins, care menus and places
- Ideas and adapted code, all under the MIT license, from the forks by
  [tubiana](https://github.com/tubiana/TamaPoke), [eperdeme](https://github.com/eperdeme/TamaPoke),
  [nzlov](https://github.com/nzlov/TamaPoke), [ShadowEnemyx](https://github.com/ShadowEnemyx/TamaPoke),
  [AlteSocke](https://github.com/AlteSocke/TamaPoke), [danielberndt](https://github.com/danielberndt/TamaPoke)
  and [Spocky2024](https://github.com/Spocky2024/TamaPoke_MrsSpock)

Tamanimals replaces all of that project's game content. Nothing from any game franchise is used here.

## Content made for Tamanimals

Generated by code in `tools/gen/` and covered by this repository's license:

- All animal sprites, thumbnails and starter art
- Ranger stamps, player avatars, backgrounds, arenas and the wordmark
- All music and animal calls
- The animal list, stats, type chart, moves, learnsets and ranger rosters

The "place" tunes are traditional, public-domain melodies: *Twinkle, Twinkle*, *Frère Jacques*, Beethoven's
*Ode to Joy* and *La Cucaracha*.
